Founder & Developer · NoteIt

2026 – present · London, UK

From live conversations to useful notes.

I’m building NoteIt: an AI platform that captures lectures and meetings, transcribes the conversation and turns audio and captured visuals into structured notes.

  • Built the web product with FastAPI, React and Supabase, with background processing, live transcription, session history and export to PDF, Word and Markdown.
  • Developed native iOS capture with audio saved locally and retried uploads to handle interruptions and poor connections.
  • Extending the project into embedded hardware and robotics prototyping, starting with NoteIt Glasses: an ESP32-S3 wearable recorder designed to hand audio to the phone app.

Glasses status: CAD frame and component placement designed. PCB routing, firmware and physical testing are still in development; no glasses have been built or measured.
